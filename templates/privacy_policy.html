Privacy Policy for sclp.pro

Effective Date: October 29, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to sclp.pro ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding that information when you use our website and tracking services.

2. Information We Collect

When you use sclp.pro, we collect the following information:

  • Roblox Account Data: When you log in using Roblox OAuth, we receive and store your public Roblox User ID and your Roblox Username.
  • Platform Progress Data: To track your obby progression, our system scans your public Roblox badge list to verify and account for in-game completions. We do not collect or scan other unrelated account data.
  • Technical Data & Server Logs: When you visit our website, our servers automatically record standard technical data. This may include your IP address, browser type, operating system, referring URLs, and interaction with our site. This information is used for security, debugging, and maintaining the stability of our service.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate your identity securely via Roblox OAuth.
  • To scan your badges and accurately display your obby progression and completions on sclp.pro.
  • To maintain, secure, and improve our platform's functionality.

4. Data Retention

Your user statistics, Roblox ID, and username are stored in our database indefinitely so that your progression on sclp.pro remains persistent and up to date.

5. Your Rights and Data Removal

You have the right to request the deletion of your data from our service at any time. If you wish to have your Roblox ID, username, and progression data completely removed from our database, you can formulate a data removal request by contacting us at [Insert Contact Email]. Upon receiving a verifiable request, we will delete your data from our systems.

You may also revoke our application's access to your Roblox account directly through your Roblox account security settings.

6. Third-Party Services

We rely on Roblox Corporation's OAuth system for user authentication and the Roblox API to fetch badge data. By using our service, you acknowledge that Roblox handles your data in accordance with their own Privacy Policy.

7. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make significant changes, we will notify users by updating the "Effective Date" at the top of this document.
