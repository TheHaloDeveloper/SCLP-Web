1. What We Collect

If you sign in with Roblox, we receive your Roblox User ID and username through Roblox OAuth (the "openid" and "profile" scopes). We get no email, password, or other account data. Our public leaderboard also lists Roblox usernames, completed towers, and a country if the member set one. We use Google Analytics and standard server logs to understand and secure the site.

2. How We Use It

To sign you in, show your username, mark your completed towers, and run the leaderboard. We do not sell your information.

3. Cookies and Storage

A session cookie keeps you signed in, and your browser saves display preferences like sort order.

4. Retention

Sign-in details are not stored in a database. They live only in your session cookie, which expires after 30 days or when you sign out. Leaderboard data is kept until you ask us to remove it.

5. Your Choices

You can sign out at any time or revoke our access in your Roblox account settings. To remove or change your leaderboard data, contact us.
